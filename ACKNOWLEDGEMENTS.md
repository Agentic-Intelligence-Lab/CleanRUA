# Acknowledgements

CleanRUA builds on the following projects. We thank their authors.

- [CleanRL](https://github.com/vwxyzjn/cleanrl): the single-file design CleanRUA follows.
- [RoboSimStudio](https://github.com/robosimstudio/robosimstudio): an inspiration for this project.
- [uni-robot-agent](https://github.com/Open-RUA/uni-robot-agent): the clean and hybrid modes behind Direct and Delegate.
- [RoboDojo](https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo): simulation tasks with two ARX X5 arms, and their official evaluator.
- [DexJoCo](https://github.com/brave-eai/dexjoco): dexterous manipulation tasks with Allegro hands, and the multi-task Pi0.5 checkpoint.
- [XPolicyLab](https://github.com/XPolicyLab/XPolicyLab): the Pi0.5 policy server for RoboDojo.
- [openpi](https://github.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi): Pi0.5 and the policy server for DexJoCo.
- [Code as Policies](https://code-as-policies.github.io/): the program-writing paradigm behind CaP.
- [PyRUA-Lean](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean): programs that call a VLA, behind `cap_delegate`.
- [RAX](https://github.com/robot-agent-exploration/rax): exploring a task and compiling it into a program, behind `cap_explore`.
- [Jev](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev): history compaction in the experimental `cap_jev` and `direct_jev`.
- [RobotUse](https://github.com/robotuse-team/RobotUse) and [Reflexion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366): playbooks across episodes in the experimental `delegate_memory`.
- [Material for MkDocs](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/): the documentation site.
